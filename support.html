Frequently asked questions

Will I lose my progress if I close the game?
Progress and settings are saved on your device. Deleting the game also deletes them.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Open the settings (gear icon) inside the game.

Why do I see ads?
Ads keep our games free. You can manage ad tracking in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

A reward ad didn't load.
Ads aren't always available. Check your internet connection and try again later.
