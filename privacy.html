Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all games and apps published by ScrptLab ("we", "us") on the App Store, except our apps made for children, which are covered by the separate Kids Apps Privacy Policy.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Our games have no accounts and no login.

Game progress (such as scores, saved games and settings like sound and vibration) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising

Some of our games show ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address (approximate location), device model, operating system and ad interaction data. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

The advertising identifier is used only if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you don't allow it, you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, our games ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Children

The games covered by this policy are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not store or retain any personal data on our servers. Data collected by advertising partners is retained according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
