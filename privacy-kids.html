Kids Apps Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all apps for children published by ScrptLab ("we", "us") on the App Store. We take children's privacy seriously and design these apps to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and Apple's App Store Kids Category guidelines.

In short: our kids apps do not collect any personal information, have no accounts or login, show no third-party ads, do not track children and do not use the device's advertising identifier.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information from children or anyone else, such as name, email address, phone number, photos, voice recordings, contacts or location.

Progress and settings (for example sound on/off) are stored only on the device and are never sent to us.

No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics

Our kids apps do not show third-party advertising, do not use the device's advertising identifier (IDFA), do not track children across apps or websites, and do not include third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Parental gate

Any links that lead outside the app (for example to this page, to the App Store or to email) and any purchases are placed behind a parental gate, so that only an adult can open them.

Parents' rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to review or delete. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us and we will respond promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
