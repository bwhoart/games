Kids Apps Support

Help for parents using our apps for children.

Contact: bekirart@icloud.com
Please include the app name, your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within 2 business days.

For parents

  • Our kids apps have no accounts, no chat, no ads and no personal data collection.
  • Links leading outside the app are protected by a parental gate.
  • Progress and settings are stored only on the device. Deleting the app also deletes them.
  • You can limit screen time with iPhone Settings › Screen Time.
  • Read our Kids Apps Privacy Policy.
